About Pexel

Pexel was developed in 1990 by the late Dr. Johnson, it was developed to help poor people in the community. In the year 2011 Pexel had already helped help 1.2 million people in Kenya. It became an international organization in the year 2014 after former President Uhuru Kenyatta introduced it as a government sponsored organization. By 2025 Pexel his working in more than 10 different countries in Africa.
